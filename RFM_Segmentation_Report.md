# Customer RFM Segmentation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This project applies Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) analysis to the Online Retail II transaction dataset.

The objective is to identify customer groups with different purchasing behaviors and provide targeted business recommendations.

The analysis produces customer-level RFM metrics, 1–5 RFM scores, practical customer segments, segment-level performance measures and recommended marketing actions.

## 2. Business Objective

The business wants to understand customer purchasing behavior and determine which customers should be:

- Retained
- Rewarded
- Reactivated
- Developed into loyal customers
- Prioritized for personalized marketing

---

## 3. Methodology

### Recency

Number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.

**Lower Recency is better.**

### Frequency

Number of unique invoices associated with the customer.

**Higher Frequency is better.**

### Monetary

Total revenue generated by the customer.

**Higher Monetary value is better.**

### RFM Scoring

Customers are divided into five groups for each RFM metric.

- Recency: scores 5 to 1, where more recent customers receive higher scores
- Frequency: scores 1 to 5, where frequent customers receive higher scores
- Monetary: scores 1 to 5, where high-spending customers receive higher scores

The total RFM score ranges from 3 to 15.

---

## 4. Data Preparation

The following cleaning steps were performed:

1. Combined all sheets in the Online Retail II workbook.
2. Removed records with missing Customer IDs.
3. Removed cancelled invoices.
4. Removed transactions with non-positive quantities.
5. Removed transactions with non-positive unit prices.
6. Converted InvoiceDate to datetime.
7. Calculated transaction revenue as Quantity × UnitPrice.

## 5. Customer Segments

### Champions

Recently active, frequent and high-value customers.

**Recommended actions:**
- VIP rewards
- Exclusive offers
- Early product access
- Personalized recommendations

### Loyal Customers

Customers with strong repeat-purchase behavior.

**Recommended actions:**
- Loyalty program rewards
- Cross-selling
- Product bundles
- Personalized promotions

### Recent Customers

Customers who have purchased recently but have not yet developed strong repeat behavior.

**Recommended actions:**
- Welcome campaigns
- Second-purchase discounts
- Product recommendations
- New-customer offers

### At Risk

Previously valuable or frequent customers who have become inactive.

**Recommended actions:**
- Win-back campaigns
- Personalized discounts
- Reminder emails
- Limited-time offers

### Lost Customers

Customers with low engagement and long periods since their last purchase.

**Recommended actions:**
- Low-cost reactivation campaigns
- Customer feedback surveys
- Carefully targeted offers
- Reduce marketing spend if repeated campaigns fail

### High Value

Customers who generate high monetary value even if they do not satisfy every Champion condition.

**Recommended actions:**
- Premium service
- VIP support
- High-value bundles
- Exclusive products

### Potential Loyalists

Customers showing promising behavior who could become stronger repeat buyers.

**Recommended actions:**
- Loyalty-program enrollment
- Personalized recommendations
- Repeat-purchase incentives
- Cross-selling

## 10. Interview Questions

### What are RFM dimensions?

RFM stands for Recency, Frequency and Monetary Value. Recency measures how recently a customer purchased, Frequency measures how often they purchase, and Monetary measures how much they spend.

### Why score customers?

Scoring customers creates a consistent framework for comparing customer behavior and assigning customers to actionable segments. It helps businesses personalize marketing, retain valuable customers and reactivate inactive customers.

---

## 11. Conclusion

RFM segmentation transforms transaction-level retail data into actionable customer groups. The approach helps a business focus retention efforts on high-value customers, develop promising customers, and selectively recover customers whose activity has declined.

The analysis can be extended with customer lifetime value, cohort analysis, churn prediction and dashboarding in Power BI.
